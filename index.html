    Questions & answers

    How working with us works

    Quick answers to the stuff people usually ask before we start a project. Tap “flip card” to see more detail.

    💻

    What types of websites do you build?

    We do single-page landings, 3–10 page business sites, and light e-commerce or booking flows. Everything is custom-designed for your brand, not a generic template.

    ⏱️

    How long does a typical project take?

    Most Starter builds ship in 1–2 weeks. Business Pro is usually 2–4 weeks. Premium Custom depends on scope, but we’ll give you a clear timeline before we start.

    🎯

    Can you redesign or improve my existing site?

    Yes. We can redesign around your current content, refresh the layout, or rebuild the whole thing while keeping your domain, SEO, and tools intact.

    📂

    What do you need from me before starting?

    A rough brief, any existing logo/brand assets, and a sense of your budget and deadline. We’ll guide you through the rest on a short kickoff call or email thread.

    🤖

    How much of the project is actually done by AI?

    We use AI for drafts, structure, and ideas — then rewrite, redesign, and clean everything by hand. You always get a human-reviewed, on-brand final result.

    💳

    How do payments work for a website project?

    Typically 50% to reserve your slot and 50% on launch. For bigger projects, we can split it into milestones so it lines up with your cash flow.
