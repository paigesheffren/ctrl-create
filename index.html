Security first

You hold the keys. We build the tools.

MetaMask is a self-custodial wallet: you control your Secret Recovery Phrase and private keys. We never have access to them.

🛡
Self-custody means your assets live on the blockchain, not on MetaMask’s servers. Safeguard your recovery phrase and never share it.
